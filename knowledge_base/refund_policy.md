# Returns and Refund Policy

We want every player to be happy with their gear. This policy explains which items can be returned, how long you have, and when your money comes back.

## Return windows by category

The return window starts on the date of delivery.

| Category | Return window | Condition required |
|---|---|---|
| Footwear (cricket shoes, running shoes, badminton shoes) | 30 days | Unworn outdoors, no spike or stud wear, original box and tags |
| Apparel (whites, jerseys, sweaters, hats, compression wear) | 30 days | Unwashed, unworn, tags attached |
| Protective gear, cricket balls, bags and accessories, other sports | 15 days | Unused, with original packaging and tags |
| Small training equipment (stumps, mallets, agility ladders) | 15 days | Unused, original packaging |
| Fitness equipment and electronics (blenders, smart band, yoga mats, bands, shakers) | 10 days | Unused and in original sealed or complete packaging |
| Cricket bats | 7 days | Not knocked-in, not oiled, no ball marks, stickers and grip intact |
| Bulky items (bowling machines, practice nets, adjustable dumbbells) | 7 days | Unused, in original packaging; return pickup by freight partner |

Defective, damaged or wrong items are handled under the Warranty and Replacements policy, not this return policy, and are always free.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Custom team jerseys and any personalised or engraved product.
- Bats that have been knocked-in (including bats sent through our knocking-in service), oiled or used.
- Abdominal guards, mouthguards and innerwear, for hygiene reasons.
- Whey protein and supplements once the seal is broken.
- Bat oil and other consumables once opened.

## How to return an item

1. Go to My Account > My Orders, select the order and choose Return.
2. Select the item, the reason, and upload photos if asked.
3. A return pickup is scheduled within 2 business days in metro cities and within 3-4 business days elsewhere. Keep the item packed in its original box.
4. If reverse pickup is not available at your PIN code, ship the item to the address shown in the return request. We reimburse up to ₹100 of courier charges as Captain's Club credit.

You can also start a return by chat, by email at support@dadasports.labspace.tech, or at any Dada Sports store with the order ID.

## Quality check

Returned items are inspected within 48 hours of reaching our fulfilment centre. If an item fails the condition check (for example, shoes worn outdoors or a bat with ball marks), we send it back to you at no cost and no refund is issued.

## Refund timelines

Refunds are initiated as soon as the returned item passes the quality check. The time for the money to reach you depends on how you paid:

| Payment method | Refund time after initiation |
|---|---|
| UPI and wallets | 1-3 business days |
| Credit and debit cards | 5-7 business days |
| Net banking | 5-7 business days |
| EMI | 5-7 business days; the EMI conversion is cancelled by your bank and any interest charged is refunded by the bank as per its policy |
| Cash on Delivery | 3-5 business days by bank transfer (NEFT) after you share your bank details, or instantly as Captain's Club store credit |
| Captain's Club credit | Instant |

You receive an email and SMS when the refund is initiated, with a reference number (ARN or UTR) you can use to check with your bank.

## How much is refunded

- The full product price is refunded for returns that pass the quality check.
- The original shipping fee (₹79), express fee (₹149) and COD handling fee (₹49) are not refunded for change-of-mind returns. They are refunded in full when the item was defective, damaged or wrong.
- If a free gift or bundled accessory is not returned with the item, its listed price is deducted from the refund.
- Captain's Club points used on the order are credited back to your points balance.

## Exchanges

Footwear and apparel can be exchanged for a different size or colour of the same product once, free of charge, within 30 days of delivery. Choose Exchange instead of Return under My Orders. The replacement size is dispatched as soon as the pickup is completed. If the requested size is out of stock, the exchange is converted to a refund.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

If you cancel an order before it is shipped, the full amount including shipping fees is refunded to the original payment method using the same timelines as above.
