# Product FAQ and Buying Guide

## English willow or Kashmir willow?

English willow is lighter, softer and more responsive, giving a bigger sweet spot and better ball pick-up. It needs knocking-in and regular oiling. Kashmir willow is harder, heavier and more durable, comes ready to play with leather balls, and suits beginners, juniors and practice sessions. Neither is suitable for hard tennis balls; use the Offside Tennis Ball Bat for tennis-ball cricket.

## Choosing bat weight

Most adult players use a bat between 1150 g and 1220 g. A lighter bat helps with back-foot and cut shots; a heavier bat suits front-foot driving. If you are unsure, choose the lighter end of the range.

## Choosing a cricket ball

- Red leather (four-piece) for long-format matches and serious practice.
- White leather for limited-overs and floodlit matches.
- Pink leather for day/night long-format matches.
- Two-piece leather balls for nets and club practice.
- Synthetic training balls for bowling machines and drills.
- Heavy tennis balls for gully and box cricket.

## Helmet sizing

Measure the head circumference about 2 cm above the eyebrows. Dada Pro helmet sizes: S 53-55 cm, M 55-57 cm, L 57-59 cm, XL 59-61 cm. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller size and adjust the rear dial. All Dada Pro helmets meet BS 7928:2013.

## Pad and glove sizing

- Boys: height under 4 ft 9 in. Youth: 4 ft 9 in to 5 ft 3 in. Men: 5 ft 3 in to 6 ft. Men Large: over 6 ft.
- Batting gloves come in right-hand and left-hand versions. A right-handed batter needs right-hand gloves (the protective side bar is on the left glove's thumb).

## Shoe size guide and spikes

Our cricket and running shoes use UK sizes. Indian sizes are the same as UK sizes. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for spike shoes to allow for thick cricket socks.

- Full metal spikes: fast bowlers and long spells on natural turf.
- Half spikes (all-rounder): batters and bowlers who play on turf.
- Rubber studs: batters, spinners, astro-turf and hard pitches.

Metal spikes can be replaced with the Spike Replacement Kit (DS-ACC-607). Tighten spikes before every match.

## Using the DS Fit BlendGo Portable Blender

- Charge fully before first use; a full charge takes 3-4 hours and gives about 15 blends.
- Add liquid first, then powder or fruit, up to the 380 ml MAX line. Small ice cubes are fine; do not blend liquids hotter than 40°C.
- Twist the jar onto the base until it clicks. The light turns blue when the safety lock is engaged; blinking red means it is not locked.
- Press the power button twice to start a 40-second blend cycle.
- Clean by blending warm water with a drop of dish soap for one cycle. Do not immerse the base in water. The jar is not dishwasher safe.

## Using the DS Fit PowerBlend 600 W

The PowerBlend runs for up to 60 seconds per cycle. Let it rest for 2 minutes between cycles. The jars and blades are dishwasher safe on the top rack; the motor base is not.

## Pairing the DS Fit Pulse Smart Band

Install the DS Fit app (Android 9 or later, iOS 15 or later), turn on Bluetooth, open the app and tap Add device. Keep the band within 1 metre of the phone. If pairing fails, restart the band by holding the side button for 10 seconds.

## Whey protein storage

Store in a cool, dry place and close the lid tightly. Use within 3 months of opening and before the best-before date printed on the jar.
