# About Dada Sports and How to Reach Us

Dada Sports is an Indian cricket and sports retailer founded in 2015 in Chennai. We sell cricket equipment, footwear, apparel, training equipment, fitness gear and equipment for other sports online at dadasports.labspace.tech, through the Dada Sports app, and at six stores. Our house brands are Dada Pro (premium cricket), Eden (club cricket), Captain (juniors), Offside (apparel and tennis-ball cricket), DS Fit (fitness), DS Run (running), DS Court (badminton) and DS Play (other sports).

## About this support assistant

The Dada Sports support assistant answers customer questions instantly, every day, in chat on the website and app and by email. It helps with orders and delivery, returns, exchanges and refunds, payments and EMI, account and login problems, product questions, warranty claims, bat care services and team orders. It answers from Dada Sports' policies and, when the customer's identity is confirmed, from their order details. When a question needs a person, such as a payment dispute, a safety issue or a request the policies do not cover, the assistant passes the conversation to the senior support team, who reply within the times listed below.

## Escalated tickets

When a ticket is escalated to our senior support team, a specialist contacts the customer within 4 business hours for urgent cases (payment problems, safety issues, legal or fraud concerns) and within 24 hours for all other cases. The customer keeps the same ticket number throughout.

## Grievance officer

In line with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, customers who are not satisfied with the resolution can write to our Grievance Officer, Ms. Ananya Roy, at grievance@dadasports.labspace.tech. Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days.
