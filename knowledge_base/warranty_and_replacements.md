# Warranty and Replacements

This policy covers items that arrive damaged, defective or wrong, and products that develop a manufacturing defect during their warranty period. Replacements and repairs under this policy are always free of charge, including pickup and return shipping.

## Damaged, defective or wrong item on delivery

If an item arrives broken, does not work out of the box, has missing parts, or is not the item you ordered:

1. Report it within 48 hours of delivery through My Orders > Report a problem, by chat, or by email to support@dadasports.labspace.tech, with your order ID.
2. Share clear photos of the product, the damage and the shipping label. For electronics and appliances (blenders, smart bands, bowling machines) and for bats, an unboxing video or a short video showing the fault helps us approve the claim faster.
3. Our team reviews the claim and responds within 24 hours.
4. Once approved, a free replacement is dispatched within 2 business days, and the courier collects the faulty item at the same time or within 2 business days. You do not have to wait for the faulty item to reach us.
5. If the same product is out of stock, we offer a full refund to the original payment method or a different product of your choice.

Reports made after 48 hours of delivery are handled as a warranty claim (below) when the product has a warranty.

## What the bat warranty covers

The bat warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use with a good-quality leather ball:

- Handle breakage or the handle coming loose at the splice.
- A crack running through the full thickness of the blade.
- Delamination of the toe that is not caused by moisture.

The bat warranty does not cover:

- Surface cracks and edge cracks. These are normal in English willow and are a sign of the willow compressing; they can usually be repaired.
- Damage from use with hard tennis balls, synthetic balls, bowling-machine balls or old, out-of-shape leather balls.
- Toe damage from moisture, or from using the bat in wet conditions without a toe guard.
- Bats that were not knocked-in before use. English willow bats must be knocked-in (see Bat Care and Services) before playing with a leather ball.
- Damage from deliberate misuse, such as hitting the ground or stumps.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Contact support with your order ID, photos of the defect and a short description of how it happened.
2. We arrange a free pickup to our service centre in Chennai, Mumbai or Bengaluru.
3. The product is inspected within 5 business days of reaching the service centre.
4. If the defect is covered, we repair the product, or replace it if a repair is not possible. If an identical replacement is not available, we issue a Captain's Club credit for the full purchase price.
5. If the damage is not covered, we offer a paid repair quote (see Bat Care and Services for bat repair prices) or return the product to you free of charge.

## Appliances and electronics: first steps before a claim

For the DS Fit BlendGo Portable Blender:

- If the blender does not start, make sure the jar is fully twisted onto the base. The magnetic safety lock prevents the blades from running unless the jar is seated correctly; a blinking red light means it is not locked.
- If it does not charge, use the supplied USB-C cable with a 5 V / 2 A adapter, and reset by holding the power button for 10 seconds.
- A burning smell during the first two uses is normal. If the blades stop, the jar cracks, or water leaks into the base, stop using the blender and raise a replacement or warranty claim.

For the DS Fit PowerBlend 600 W: if the motor stops during use, the overload protection has tripped. Unplug for 20 minutes before trying again. If it does not restart, raise a warranty claim.

## Product safety incidents (smoke, sparks, overheating, injury)

If any product, especially an electrical appliance such as a blender, smart band charger or bowling machine, smokes, sparks, overheats, smells of burning plastic, or causes an injury:

1. Stop using it immediately. Switch it off and unplug it, or disconnect the charging cable, if it is safe to do so. Do not try to open or repair it.
2. If anyone is hurt, get medical help first. For burns, cool the area under cool running water for at least 20 minutes.
3. Keep the product and its packaging; do not throw it away. Our product safety team may need to inspect it.
4. Report it to us by chat or by email with your order ID, photos or a short video, and a description of what happened.

Safety reports are handled by our product safety team, which contacts the customer within 4 business hours. The faulty unit is collected free of charge, and the customer receives a full refund or a free replacement after the report is reviewed. Products involved in a safety incident are never repaired and returned.

## Replacement versus refund for defective items

Within the return window, you can choose either a replacement or a full refund for a defective item. After the return window, defective items under warranty are repaired or replaced; a refund is offered only if neither is possible.
