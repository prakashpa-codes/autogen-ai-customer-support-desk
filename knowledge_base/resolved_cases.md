# Previously Resolved Support Cases

These are real-world patterns from tickets that the senior support team resolved and approved for reuse. Each case lists the problem, the resolution and the policy it relied on. Policies remain the source of truth; these cases show how the policies are applied.

## Case: Parcel stuck at a courier hub

A customer in Pune reported that their order had shown "In transit - Bhiwandi hub" for 4 business days and was 3 business days past its Estimated Delivery Date. Support raised a priority trace with the courier and updated the customer within 24 hours. The parcel had been mis-sorted; it was re-routed and delivered 2 business days later. Because the order arrived more than 5 business days after the EDD, the customer was offered the ₹150 Captain's Club late delivery credit. Policy: Shipping and Delivery Policy.

## Case: Charged twice on UPI for one order

A customer saw ₹3,299 debited twice for one PowerBlend order. Only one order existed in My Orders. Support explained that the second debit was a pending authorisation, which the bank released automatically on the fourth business day. No refund action was needed from Dada Sports. Policy: Billing and Payments, duplicate charges.

## Case: Toe crack on a new English willow bat

A customer reported a crack at the toe of an Eden Grade 3 bat two weeks after delivery. Photos showed moisture swelling at the toe and no toe guard. The damage was not covered by the warranty (moisture damage), so support offered the paid toe repair and toe guard service at ₹499 with free return shipping as a goodwill gesture. The customer accepted. Policy: Warranty and Replacements, bat warranty exclusions; Bat Care and Services.

## Case: Handle came loose on a Dada Pro bat

The handle of a Dada Pro Grade 1 bat came loose at the splice after 3 months of normal use. This is a covered manufacturing defect within the 6-month warranty. The bat was picked up free of charge, the handle was replaced at the Chennai service centre, and the bat was returned within 9 business days. Policy: Warranty and Replacements.

## Case: BlendGo blender not starting on arrival

A customer said their new BlendGo Portable Blender would not start. Support walked them through seating the jar until the light turned blue; the light kept blinking red, which showed a faulty safety sensor. The claim was raised within 48 hours of delivery with a short video, approved the same day, and a replacement was dispatched within 2 business days with pickup of the faulty unit. Policy: Warranty and Replacements, damaged or defective on delivery.

## Case: Wrong shoe size

A customer ordered Eden Rubber Stud Cricket Shoes in UK 8 and found them tight. Support converted the request into a free size exchange to UK 9 under My Orders. The new pair was dispatched when the pickup was completed. Policy: Returns and Refund Policy, exchanges.

## Case: OTP not arriving during login

A customer could not log in because the OTP never arrived. They had requested 5 OTPs within the hour, which triggered the hourly limit. Support explained the 60-minute wait and suggested resetting the password through the email reset link instead, which worked immediately. Policy: Account and Login Help.

## Case: COD order refund

A customer returned COD-paid running shoes. After the quality check, support sent the secure link to submit bank details, and the refund reached the customer by NEFT within 4 business days. Policy: Returns and Refund Policy, refund timelines.
