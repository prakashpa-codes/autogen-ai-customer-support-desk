# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods

- UPI (any UPI app)
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay and American Express
- Net banking from 50+ Indian banks
- Wallets: Paytm, PhonePe and Amazon Pay
- Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders up to ₹10,000, with a ₹49 handling fee
- EMI on credit cards (see below)
- Captain's Club store credit and points

All online payments are processed by our RBI-authorised payment gateway. Dada Sports never stores your full card number or CVV.

## EMI options

- No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months on orders of ₹3,000 or more, using HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI or Axis Bank credit cards. The interest is given back to you as an upfront discount.
- Standard EMI for 9 or 12 months on orders of ₹2,500 or more, on credit cards from most major banks, at the bank's interest rate.
- EMI conversion is done by your bank and can take up to 3 business days to show on your card statement.

## Charged more than once (duplicate or multiple charges)

If you see the same amount debited two or more times:

1. Check My Orders. If only one order was created, the extra debits are usually pending authorisations held by your bank. These are released automatically by the bank within 5-7 business days and no action is needed from you.
2. If more than one order was created by mistake, cancel the extra orders under My Orders before they ship. The money is refunded to the original payment method in 5-7 business days (1-3 business days for UPI).
3. If the extra amount was actually captured (it appears as a settled transaction, not pending, after 2 business days), contact support with the order ID, the transaction reference numbers (UTR for UPI, last 4 digits for cards) and a screenshot of the bank statement. Our Payments team verifies the capture and refunds every duplicate charge within 48 hours of verification.

## Payment failed but money was debited

If the payment failed or the order was not created but your account was debited, the amount is refunded automatically to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. If you have not received it after 7 business days, contact support with the transaction reference number.

## Payment disputes and chargebacks

If you have raised a chargeback or dispute with your bank, our Payments team handles the case directly with the bank. While a chargeback is open, we cannot issue a separate refund for the same transaction, so that you are not refunded twice. Disputes are answered to your bank within the timeline set by the card network, usually within 10 business days.

## Unauthorised or fraudulent transactions

If a payment was made from your card or account without your permission:

1. Call your bank immediately to block the card or UPI ID.
2. Report it to us. We freeze the Dada Sports account involved, cancel any unshipped orders, and investigate within 3 business days.
3. You may also report cyber fraud on the national helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

## Invoices and GST

- A GST invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched and can be downloaded from My Orders at any time.
- To claim input tax credit, enter your company name and GSTIN at checkout. A GSTIN cannot be added after the invoice has been generated.
- Prices on the website include GST.

## Coupons and offers

- Only one coupon can be applied per order.
- Coupons cannot be applied to custom team jerseys or bowling machines unless the coupon says so.
- If you return part of an order that used a coupon, the coupon discount is shared across the items in proportion to their price, and only the paid amount for the returned item is refunded.

## Captain's Club points

- Members earn 1 point for every ₹100 spent (after discounts).
- 1 point is worth ₹1. Points can be redeemed in multiples of 100, with a minimum of 200 points, for up to 20% of the order value.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Points used on a returned or cancelled order are credited back.
