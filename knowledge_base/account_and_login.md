# Account and Login Help

A Dada Sports account lets you track orders, start returns, save addresses and earn Captain's Club points. You can sign in on the website or the Dada Sports app (Android and iOS) with the same account.

## Creating an account

Sign up with your mobile number or email address. Mobile sign-up is verified with a 6-digit one-time password (OTP); email sign-up is verified with a link. Guest checkout is available, but guest orders cannot earn Captain's Club points and must be tracked with the order ID and email address.

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include at least one number and one special character. Your last 3 passwords cannot be reused.

## Resetting a forgotten password

1. On the Login page, select Forgot password.
2. Enter the email address or mobile number registered with your account.
3. We send a 6-digit OTP to your mobile (valid for 10 minutes) or a reset link to your email (valid for 30 minutes).
4. Enter the OTP or open the link, then choose a new password that follows the password rules.
5. After a successful reset you are signed out of all other devices for your security. Sign in again with the new password.

If you no longer have access to the registered email or mobile number, contact support. We verify your identity using a recent order ID and the delivery PIN code before updating the contact details.

## Signing in with OTP instead of a password

You can choose Login with OTP on the Login page. The OTP is sent to your registered mobile number and is valid for 10 minutes.

## Not receiving the OTP

- Wait 60 seconds before tapping Resend OTP.
- Check that the mobile number is correct and has network coverage.
- If Do Not Disturb (DND) is enabled with your operator, OTP messages are still delivered, but may be delayed by a few minutes. Our sender ID is DADASP.
- Check the spam folder if you chose email.
- You can request at most 5 OTPs per hour. After that, wait 60 minutes before requesting again.

## Account locked after failed attempts

After 5 incorrect password or OTP attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. You can wait for 30 minutes, or unlock immediately by resetting your password with Forgot password.

## Changing your email address or mobile number

Go to My Account > Profile. Changing the mobile number requires an OTP sent to both the old and the new number. Changing the email requires clicking a confirmation link sent to the new email. If you no longer have the old number, contact support for identity verification.

## Two-step verification

You can turn on two-step verification under My Account > Security. When it is on, new devices need both your password and an OTP.

## Suspicious activity on your account

If you see an order, address or login that you do not recognise:

1. Reset your password immediately.
2. Use Sign out of all devices under My Account > Security.
3. Contact support. We temporarily freeze the account, cancel any unshipped orders you did not place, and investigate within 3 business days.

Dada Sports will never ask for your password, OTP, card PIN or CVV by phone, email, chat or WhatsApp.

## Deleting your account

You can request deletion under My Account > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is completed after a 30-day cooling-off period, during which you can cancel the request by signing in. Unused Captain's Club points and store credit are forfeited when the account is deleted. Order invoices are retained for 8 years as required by Indian tax law.
