# Team, Club and Academy Orders

Dada Sports supplies cricket clubs, schools, colleges and academies across India. Team orders are handled by our Teams desk at teams@dadasports.labspace.tech (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM IST).

## Custom team jerseys

- Fully sublimated jerseys with your team name, logo, player names and numbers (product DS-APP-504).
- Price: ₹899 per jersey, minimum order 11 jerseys.
- Process: send the design brief and logo (vector or high-resolution PNG), receive a digital mock-up within 2 business days, approve it, and pay a 50% advance.
- Production time: 10-14 business days after mock-up approval, plus normal delivery time.
- Custom jerseys cannot be cancelled after production starts and cannot be returned. Printing defects reported within 7 days of delivery are reprinted free of charge.

## Bulk equipment orders

For orders of 11 or more units of the same product (bats, pads, balls, helmets, kit bags), the Teams desk sends a quotation within 2 business days.

| Order size (units of one product) | Team discount |
|---|---|
| 11-25 | 10% |
| 26-50 | 15% |
| 51 or more | 20% |

Team discounts cannot be combined with coupons. A GST invoice in the institution's name is issued; enter the GSTIN when the order is placed.

## Payment terms for institutions

Schools, colleges and registered academies can pay by bank transfer (NEFT or RTGS) against a proforma invoice. Orders above ₹1,00,000 can be paid 50% in advance and 50% before dispatch.

## Academy partner programme

Registered academies with at least 30 enrolled players can join the Academy Partner Programme: a dedicated account manager, a 12% discount on all non-custom products for enrolled players who shop with the academy code, and free knocking-in for bats bought through the programme.

## Delivery for team orders

Team orders ship from the nearest fulfilment centre. Orders weighing more than 30 kg travel by freight partner and take 2 extra business days. Delivery to a school or ground address is available; someone must be present to sign for the delivery.
