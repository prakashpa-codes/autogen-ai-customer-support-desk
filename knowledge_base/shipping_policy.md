# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Dada Sports ships to every serviceable PIN code in India from three fulfilment centres: Sriperumbudur (Chennai, Tamil Nadu), Bhiwandi (Maharashtra) and Hoskote (Bengaluru, Karnataka). Orders are routed to the nearest centre that has every item in stock.

## Dispatch times

- Orders placed and paid before 2:00 PM IST, Monday to Saturday, are dispatched the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, or on Sundays and national holidays, are dispatched the next business day.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) orders are dispatched after an automated confirmation call or WhatsApp confirmation, usually within 2 hours.
- Made-to-order items (custom team jerseys, engraved bats) and bats sent for our knocking-in service have their own production time, shown on the product page, which is added before dispatch.

## Delivery timelines

Delivery timelines are counted in business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays) from the day of dispatch.

| Destination | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Metro cities: Kolkata, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad | 2-4 business days | Next business day if ordered before 12:00 noon IST |
| Tier 2 cities and state capitals | 3-6 business days | Not available |
| Rest of India | 5-8 business days | Not available |
| North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep | 7-12 business days | Not available |

Bulky items (bowling machines, practice nets, adjustable dumbbells) travel by our freight partner and take 2 extra business days on top of the standard timeline.

The Estimated Delivery Date (EDD) shown at checkout and in the order confirmation email is the date we commit to.

## Shipping charges

- Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Orders below ₹999 have a shipping fee of ₹79.
- Express shipping (metro cities only) costs ₹149 per order, regardless of order value.
- Captain tier members of the Captain's Club loyalty programme get free express shipping on every order.
- Cash on Delivery has an additional handling fee of ₹49. COD is available for orders up to ₹10,000.

## Courier partners and tracking

We ship with Blue Dart, Delhivery and Ekart. Remote PIN codes may be served by India Post Speed Post.

- A tracking link is sent by SMS, email and WhatsApp as soon as the order is dispatched.
- You can also track the order under My Account > My Orders > Track.
- Tracking usually starts showing scans within 24 hours of dispatch.

## My order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date

If your order has not arrived by the Estimated Delivery Date (EDD):

1. Up to 1 business day after the EDD: check the tracking link. Most parcels that are slightly late are still moving and are delivered within 1 business day.
2. 2 or more business days after the EDD: contact support with your order ID. We raise a priority trace with the courier and update you within 24 hours.
3. If the parcel shows no tracking movement for 7 calendar days after the EDD, we declare it lost in transit and offer you a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not need to wait for the courier's investigation to finish.

Late delivery goodwill credit: if an order is delivered more than 5 business days after its EDD because of a delay on our side or the courier's side, you can request a ₹150 Captain's Club credit. The credit is added to your account within 2 business days of the request.

## Failed delivery attempts

The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After 3 failed attempts the parcel is returned to our fulfilment centre (Return to Origin, RTO).

- For prepaid orders returned to origin, we refund the full product amount within 5-7 business days of the parcel reaching us. The shipping fee is not refunded if the delivery failed because the address was incorrect or the customer was unreachable.
- For COD orders returned to origin, the order is cancelled. Repeated COD refusals (3 or more) may disable COD on the account.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed free of charge until the order status is Packed. After the order is Packed or Shipped, the address cannot be changed; you may refuse delivery and place a new order, and the refused order is refunded as an RTO.

## Damaged, tampered or incomplete parcels

If the outer package is visibly damaged or tampered with at delivery, you may refuse it. If you have accepted it, report damage, missing items or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the package and product. See the Warranty and Replacements policy for the replacement process.

## Store pickup

Click and Collect is available at all six Dada Sports stores. Choose Store Pickup at checkout; the order is ready for collection within 4 hours (in-stock items) and held for 7 days. Bring the order ID and a photo ID.
