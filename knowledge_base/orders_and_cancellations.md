# Orders and Cancellations

## Order statuses explained

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Placed | We have received your order and payment (or COD confirmation is pending). |
| Confirmed | Payment is verified and the order is assigned to a fulfilment centre. |
| Packed | Items are packed and waiting for courier pickup. Address and size changes are no longer possible. |
| Shipped | The courier has collected the parcel. A tracking link is sent by SMS, email and WhatsApp. |
| Out for delivery | The parcel is with the delivery agent and will arrive today. |
| Delivered | The courier has marked the parcel as delivered. |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled by you or by us; any payment is refunded. |
| Return requested / Return picked up / Refund initiated | Stages of a return. |

An order that stays in Placed for more than 30 minutes after a UPI payment usually means the payment confirmation from the bank is delayed. It is updated automatically within 2 hours; if the payment fails, the amount is refunded within 5-7 business days.

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel any order free of charge until it is Shipped, under My Orders > Cancel. The full amount, including shipping fees, is refunded.
- After the order is Shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery, or accept it and start a return within the return window.
- Made-to-order items (custom team jerseys, engraved bats) can be cancelled only within 24 hours of placing the order, before production starts.
- Bats booked for our knocking-in service can be cancelled until the service starts, which is shown as Knocking-in started in My Orders.

## Orders cancelled by Dada Sports

We may cancel an order if an item turns out to be out of stock after the order is placed, if the PIN code becomes unserviceable, or if the order fails a payment security check. You are informed by SMS and email, and the full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

## Changing an order

- Size, colour or quantity changes are possible until the order is Packed. Contact support with the order ID.
- The delivery address can be changed until the order is Packed (see Shipping and Delivery Policy).
- Items cannot be added to an existing order; place a new order instead. Orders placed within 30 minutes of each other to the same address are shipped together where possible.

## Partial shipments

If items in one order are stocked at different fulfilment centres, they may arrive in separate parcels with separate tracking links. Each parcel has its own Estimated Delivery Date, shown in My Orders.

## Pre-orders and back-orders

Items marked Pre-order show an expected dispatch date on the product page. Pre-orders are charged at the time of ordering and can be cancelled free of charge at any time before dispatch.

## Order confirmation and invoices

An order confirmation is sent by email and SMS within 15 minutes of placing the order. If you did not receive it, check My Orders; if the order is listed there, it is confirmed. A GST invoice is emailed on dispatch.

## Finding your order ID

Your order ID starts with ORD- followed by digits (for example ORD-48213). It appears in the confirmation email, SMS, and under My Account > My Orders.
